# screen48

The Sibyl 1.4 learning diagnostic: six authored development tasks in two families
(interval_capacity, utf8_framing), four memory arms (native, raw_retrieval,
strong_summary, no_memory), two checkpoints (0 = before one complete ordinary
consolidation cycle over the 233 admitted captures, 1 = after). 48 cells.

Layout:

- `contract.py`: fixed policy (tasks, arms, budgets, headers, material bindings).
- `recall/`: pack preparation over product recall and search owners.
- `material/`: frozen policy inputs, the 233-source geometry, the summary library.
- `schedule/`: the frozen 48-cell schedule and task bindings.
- `cycle.py`: drives the product dream job until every original is terminal.
- `checkpoints.py`: native inventory snapshots and pack preparation per checkpoint.
- `materialize.py`: one-task-per-checkpoint manifests over `benchmarks.agent_tasks.manifest`.
- `launcher.py`: 48-row executor with atomic claim and per-cell receipts.
- `observation.py`: the observation-only report over the 48-row schedule.
- `devbox/`: staging, preflight and phase runners for the eval host.

Task material for the transfer tasks lives in `benchmarks/agent_tasks/transfer_material/`.
