A missing or failed map keeps its family incomplete; a failed reduction also keeps its family incomplete. Preserve all partial outputs, physical usage and unknown outcomes. ## Why preserve family references

The 20 corpus-wide groups save eight map calls but mix family content. Their outputs could be retained as an alternative retrieval library; a single global reducer is not required. Compressing all 233 experiences into one tiny final summary would introduce a concentration of information loss and make retrieval comparisons harder to interpret.

The 28-map family layout spends eight more calls to preserve useful reference boundaries already present in training metadata. Its eight reductions produce one artifact per family. The summary arm sees multiple native pages together, and its processor allowance is larger than the proposed ordinary 92,000-character setting. Report those construction differences and actual input/output usage. This experiment compares complete memory pipelines, not identical processor request boundaries.

The shared source views omit transport and audit-only fields under the existing projection receipt. They are complete semantic controller views, not the full raw archive. Both representations remain recoverable from immutable originals. Summary completion is coverage and receipt acceptance, not proof that the model preserved every important lesson.
