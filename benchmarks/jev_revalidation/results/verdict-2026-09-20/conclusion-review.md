## Suggestions not adopted

Pooling selected, repeated assertions across study cohorts would not estimate a
production error rate. Multiplying such a rate by deliberately adversarial hint
harm would additionally assume a natural error distribution the experiments did
not measure. Historical disagreements are reported separately instead.

A serial adviser call does not mathematically preclude lower total latency: the
consumer's work can change after receiving advice. The frozen speed target is
retained as historical protocol, without treating its failure as the sole reason
to defer activation. No claim of statistical equivalence replaces the observed
cost difference.

The review described disabling experimental arms as leaving production on the
prefix baseline. The study did not establish that production uses that baseline.
The final report states this boundary explicitly.

The reviewer checked selected arithmetic and annotations but did not independently
verify the complete numerical audit, replay archives, preflight or 425-test suite.
Those checks have separate receipts. The external review was an interpretation
challenge, not a new blinded semantic annotation pass.
